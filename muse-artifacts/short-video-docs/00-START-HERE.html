Short-Video Project — Start Here

Last updated: 2026-09-25

Goal

Build and operate an automated short-video feed across multiple themed channels (YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, Facebook Reels, TikTok), starting with Channel #1: AI Technology Education. All generated media is original — never pulled from the internet without disclosure and approval.

The five pipelines

Each is an independent codebase with its own bug/failure/performance tracking, self-healing, and alerting. They share one learning knowledge base.

# Pipeline Owns
1 create-and-publish Idea → generation → QC → publish, behind per-video approval during the pilot
2 promotion Organic amplification + paid ladder (probe → validate → scale). Objective: min cost and max impact
3 housekeeping Monthly stale-content review, ledger hygiene, link checks
4 performance-monitoring Per-pipeline stats collection, shared reporting framework, R1/R2/R3 cross-pipeline rollups, anomaly detection
5 earnings-improvement Revenue attribution, affiliate optimization, sponsor pipeline

See reference/architecture.md for the full architecture.
