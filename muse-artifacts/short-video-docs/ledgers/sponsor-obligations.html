Sponsor Obligations Ledger

Last updated: 2026-09-25. One row per sponsored video. Maintained by the earnings-improvement pipeline; housekeeping reads it for the monthly review.

Gating rule

No action to hide, unlist, archive, trim, or materially alter a video is permitted while its obligations remain active, unless explicitly approved under the contract. The stale-content remediation workflow must check this ledger first.
