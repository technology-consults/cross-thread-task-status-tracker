Topic Engine Scope

Last updated: 2026-09-24.

Candidate sources

The pipeline autonomously identifies candidate topics from:

  • Official product and feature announcements.
  • New tools.
  • Pricing and cost changes.
  • Announced launches and event calendars.
  • Search and platform trends.
  • Performance data from prior videos (via the shared learning knowledge base).

Selection rules

  • Dedup: every candidate is compared against the full content ledger; near-duplicates are rejected.
  • Variation: episode-to-episode variation must be meaningful — no template-churned repeats of the same topic.
  • Guardrails first: universal content guardrails are enforced at ideation, before any scripting (see reference/content-guardrails.md).
  • News-reactive, not predictive: the engine reacts to unannounced news as it breaks; it never pretends to predict events.

Editorial scope (AI Technology Education)

  • In scope: product launches, feature announcements, new tools, pricing/cost changes.
  • Out of scope: negative reputational news, scandals, controversies.
  • Sensitive or reputational-risk topics are flagged for BalRam's call before publishing.

Approval flow

  • Pilot: candidates and finished videos route through BalRam's per-video approval gate.
  • Full auto: only after BalRam authorizes it ("go auto" / "lock the vision"). The engine then runs idea → publication without routine human handling, within the guardrails and scope above.
