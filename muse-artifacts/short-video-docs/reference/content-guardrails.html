Content Guardrails

Locked 2026-09-24. Apply to ALL current and future niches and channels.

Universal guardrails

  • No sensitive or objectionable content.
  • Nothing involving children.
  • No current politics.
  • Nothing potentially controversial or otherwise inappropriate.

Enforcement

  • The topic engine enforces these rules at ideation, before generation.
  • Violations are rejected outright, or flagged for BalRam's review when borderline.
  • No video proceeds to scripting while a guardrail flag is unresolved.

News-reactive scoping (AI Technology Education)

In-scope:

  • Product launches and feature announcements.
  • New tools.
  • Pricing and cost changes.

Out of scope:

  • Negative reputational news about companies or people.
  • Scandals and controversies.
  • Anything that risks reputational harm to the channel.

Sensitive or reputational-risk topics are flagged for BalRam's call before publishing — never published on the pipeline's own judgment.

Review note

During the pilot, every candidate and video additionally routes through BalRam's per-video approval gate, which is a second enforcement layer on top of the automated rules.
